# ⁉ Start: Where *am* I?

You sit up suddenly, blinking and rubbing at your eyes in confusion. You expect to see the dimly let comfort of your bedroom around you but instead, you're greeted by scenery you've never seen before. 

Harsh yellows, beiges, and tans assult your senses. The carpet underneath you is musty and matted, stained from years of use. The walls are peeling in spots and the flouescent lights crackle above you. 

> 💭 I don't think I partied *that hard* last night... surely I would have remembered coming to a place like this.

Everything you're seeing is contradictory. It's both *too bright* and *unnaturally dim*. The space is both *spwaling and massive* -- a mess of hallways and doors that make no logical sense, and *suffocatingly small* -- cielings so low you could probably touch them if you tried. 

You stand up, grabbing your backpack from nearby. It had been next to your bed when you went to sleep last night. There's not much of use inside. A notebook, some pens, your water bottle, a few granola bars, your phone and charger. 

You eaggerly pull your phone out only for it to flash "no service". There doesn't seem to be internet here either. 

> 💭 Strange. What sort of office doesn't have internet?

At least... you're *assuming* this is an office. It almost looks like one... if offices were designed to look like a lab rat's maze.
