# 🧭 Scene 7A: The Landmarks are Moving

You decide to try to use landmarks to navigate. 

No matter how hard you try, landmarks you're sure you've identified seem to shift and move around you. You end up turned around, confused, and just as lost as you were before. 

You find a single metal folding chair in the center of an open room, and decide to flip it upside-down. It'll be more obvious that you've been here the next time you see it. 

You set off again, being sure you're walking away from the chair. Yet somehow -- impossibly -- you see the familiar shape in the distance. The hallway you're walking down opens up to reveal the same room you had just been in; the chair sitting upside-down the same way you'd left it.

You let out a frustrated sigh. 

> 💭 Clearly the landmarks can't be trusted either. If this place can't be mapped, and I can't get out using landmarks... what am I suposed to do?

You take a deep breath and grab the straps of your backpack to steady yourself. 

- 😱 [I am actually freaking out right now.]()

- 😱 [Take a deep breath, figure out what my options are.]()
