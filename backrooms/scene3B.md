# 🧭 Scene 3B: Where are you going?

As you walk, you pass through a doorway. You realize that the halls you've grown so accoustomed to no longer look the same. 

It's a dimly lit hallway, with two open doors on either side of the hall. You squint your eyes, trying to see into the blinding light that pours from each of the rooms without actually entering either. 

One of the rooms seems to open up into a sprawling massive indoor waterpark. Slick white and blue tiles cover the pool deck. Brightly coloured plastic slides twist through the air and disapear into the walls. The air in there seems thick and heavy; a warm and uncomfrotable chlorine smell lingers in the air sickeningly. Somehow, the scent of suncreen and fresh towels permiates the air as well. You see no one, but if you listen hard enough you *swear* you can hear kids laughing and playing in the distance. 

The other room opens up to a darkly lit street. The air is heavy with the scent of nostalgia and your hometown. All of the houses nearby are dark; their windows vacant and empty. They sit like sleeping beasts amongst the landscape. However, off in the distance, there is a single home. It lies illuminated in the warm glow of incandesant lights. You can almost see that the front door is cracked open, beconing you home. After all, it is your childhood home sitting on the horizon; waiting for you to return. It always knew you would. 

*What do you do?*

- 😱 [I panic. *HOW* is everything changing around me?]()

- 🏡 [I walk towards my childhood home.]()

- 🌊 [I walk towards the water park.]()
